The Association

About WeldNAM

The Association for Welding and Allied Processes of Namibia (WeldNAM) serves as the country's representative member society in the International Institute of Welding (IIW).

In partnership with the University of Namibia, WeldNAM delivers the International Welding Engineer (IWE) qualification, a globally recognised credential that strengthens workforce competence across Namibia's industrial sectors.
