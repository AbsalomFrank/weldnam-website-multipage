Setting the standard for welding excellence

International Welding Engineer (IWE) qualification, Namibia

THE FIRST NAMIBIAN INTERNATIONAL WELDING ENGINEERS (IWE) COHORT

The IWE programme targets Namibia's key growth sectors, giving employers qualified welding engineers who meet international standards.

⚡

Green Hydrogen

Specialised welding engineering training for Namibia's emerging green hydrogen sector and related infrastructure.

🛢

Oil & Gas

Meeting the rigorous welding quality requirements for oil and gas pipeline and facility construction in Namibia.

⛏

Mining & Construction

Qualified IWEs for Namibia's mining sector, shipbuilding, and large-scale steel construction projects.
