IWE programme

International Welding Engineer qualification, delivered in Namibia with the University of Namibia.

Namibia's first International Welding Engineer cohort

The programme trains welding engineers for the sectors below. Employers get people who meet the IIW qualification.

  • Green hydrogen

    Welding engineering for hydrogen infrastructure.

  • Oil and gas

    Welding quality for pipeline and facility construction.

  • Mining and construction

    Mining, shipbuilding and steel construction.

Recognition and scholarships

IIW accreditation

The qualification is accredited through the IIW and recognised across Europe, Africa, and beyond.

Sectors

Training is aimed at green hydrogen, oil and gas, mining, and steel construction.

40 scholarships

Fully funded over three years, covering tuition, transport, accommodation, and meals.
