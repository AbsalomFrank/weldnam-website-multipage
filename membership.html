Membership

Join WeldNAM

Membership in WeldNAM is open to any natural person or organisation that shares the association's objectives. Members pledge their support and contribute to:

  • Pursuing welding and allied processes in Namibia through research, education, training, qualification and certification
  • Fostering welding as a key technology in Namibia's industrialisation
  • Participating in international standardisation and guideline-setting in welding
  • Representing Namibia in welding and allied processes, nationally and internationally
  • Establishing and maintaining a quality and certification infrastructure for welding in Namibia
